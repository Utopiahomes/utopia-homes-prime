## Purpose of this meeting

Walk a prospective owner through how Utopia Homes would bring their home onto its vacation-rental
program, agree on what information is still needed, and leave with clear next steps. Nothing
said in this meeting is a signed agreement.

## What Utopia Homes does for owners

Utopia combines local ownership, vacation-rental management, distinctive marketing, and
hands-on property stewardship. The owner model focuses on earning potential, day-to-day
operations, local oversight, and thoughtful improvements. Current homes are large-group stays in
Wildwood, North Wildwood, and Wildwood Crest, New Jersey.

## Onboarding stages (illustrative demo flow)

*These stages illustrate a possible onboarding flow for this demo. They are not a confirmed
Utopia operating process.*

1. **Introductory meeting** — understand the home, the owner's goals, and any constraints
   (owner-use weeks, house rules, neighbours, HOA).
2. **Property review** — walkthrough of the home, condition, furnishings, and guest capacity.
3. **Information gathering** — complete the Owner Onboarding Checklist.
4. **Management agreement** — terms, fees, and start date are handled in a separate written
   agreement *(to confirm: agreement owner and review process)*.
5. **Readiness** — photography, listing content, guest supplies, and access setup.
6. **Launch** — listing goes live on the configured booking channels.

## What the owner can expect from Utopia

- A named Utopia contact during onboarding *(to confirm: who)*.
