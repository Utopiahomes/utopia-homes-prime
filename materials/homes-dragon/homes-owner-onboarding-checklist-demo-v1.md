# Utopia Homes Owner Onboarding Checklist — Demo v1

> **Demo material. Pending Ray's approval.** Use this list to identify missing information.
> Do not collect bank, payment, or government ID details in the meeting.

## Owner and ownership

- [ ] Owner name(s) and preferred contact method
- [ ] Who can make decisions about the home (all owners, a representative, a company)
- [ ] Confirmation the owner has the right to rent the home *(document type to confirm)*

## The home

- [ ] Address and property type
- [ ] Bedrooms, bathrooms, and beds; maximum guest capacity
- [ ] Parking spaces and any street or permit rules
- [ ] Current condition and any planned repairs or improvements
- [ ] Furnishings and what stays with the home

## Rules and restrictions

- [ ] HOA, condo, or community rules that affect short-term rental
- [ ] Local rental licence or registration status *(requirements to confirm per municipality)*
- [ ] House rules the owner wants (pets, events, quiet hours, minimum age)

## Availability

- [ ] Owner-use dates to block
- [ ] Earliest date the home could be ready for guests
- [ ] Existing bookings or commitments

## Operations

- [ ] Access method (keys, smart lock, lockbox)
- [ ] Utilities and internet provider details
- [ ] Current cleaners, maintenance contacts, or vendors to keep
- [ ] Insurance: current policy type and whether it covers short-term rental *(to confirm)*

## Marketing

- [ ] Existing photos, or a date for professional photography
- [ ] Features the owner wants highlighted
- [ ] Anything that must not appear in listings

## Next steps (filled in during the meeting)

- [ ] Proposed walkthrough date
- [ ] Who sends the management agreement, and when
- [ ] Open questions to resolve before the agreement
